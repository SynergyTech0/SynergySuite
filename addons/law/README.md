# Synergy Law

A law practice add-on: matters, conflict checks, court deadlines, time and bills, and client trust accounts, with billing and trust posting into Books. Ported from SynergyLaw onto the suite's shared packages. Case files live only in each firm's own database; none ship with the code.

- **Clients and matters.** A matter has a number (`2026-0001`), a client, a practice area, the jurisdiction whose rules apply, court and case number, fee terms (hourly, flat, contingency or pro bono), the engagement letter date, a team with each person's rate, and the parties on every side. Every change is kept in the matter's history.
- **Conflict checks.** A new matter starts with a check on the client and everyone else involved. Names are evened out before comparing (case, punctuation, Inc./Incorporated, "Doe, Jane" and "Jane Doe", d/b/a and a/k/a names as aliases) and scored against every client and every party on every matter in the firm, so one-letter typos still match. A check with matches needs a decision before the matter opens: cleared, waived (with the consent noted) or declined (with a reason).
- **Deadlines.** Per matter and firm-wide, with a calculator for FRCP and FRAP periods: calendar or court days, three extra days for mail service, and a last day on a weekend or court holiday runs to the next court day. Federal holidays are built in; `LAW_COURT_HOLIDAYS` adds local closures. AI-read documents can add the deadlines they mention.
- **Time and bills.** Time and expenses at each person's rate on the matter (else the matter's rate). The responsible lawyer bills unbilled work (a flat fee goes on the first bill); with Books connected each bill becomes a Books draft invoice.
- **Trust accounts.** Deposits, payments out and earned-fee transfers against a bill. No client or matter can go below zero, entries are never edited or deleted, only voided with a reason, and each account reconciles three ways (bank statement, ledger, sum of client balances). Late deposits and overdue reconciliations are flagged against the state's rule. Only trust keepers (`LAW_TRUST_EMAILS`, default the Law admins, else everyone) can record entries.
- **Books.** With `BOOKS_SVC_TOKEN` set (or `LAW_BOOKS_TOKEN`), every trust entry and void posts to Books as a balanced journal entry: deposits Dr trust bank (`LAW_BOOKS_TRUST_BANK`, 1015) / Cr client trust liability (`LAW_BOOKS_TRUST_LIABILITY`, 2150); earned fees also move Dr operating (`LAW_BOOKS_OPERATING`, 1010) / Cr receivables (`LAW_BOOKS_RECEIVABLE`, 1100). Add the trust accounts to Books' chart first. A posting that fails is kept and can be retried.
- **State rules.** A guide for all 50 states and DC: deposit timing, reconciliation interval, record keeping years, when a fee needs a written agreement, and contingency limits, with a link to each state's rules. Matters are checked against it. These are a working guide, not legal advice; confirm against the rule for your state.
- **Documents.** PDF, Word and text files up to 50 MB, kept in the database. Text is read out for search. On the suite's AI model (`@synergy/ai`; `LAW_AI_*` to give Law its own), a document can be summarized (type, date, parties, key points, deadlines, timeline) and a matter given an overview from its documents. Both are a first pass for a person.
- **Access.** A matter is visible to its responsible lawyer, its team, and the Law admins (`LAW_ADMIN_EMAILS`, default `ADMIN_EMAILS`). Conflict checks see every client and party firm-wide by design.
- **Suite search** (`POST /api/search`, per person) and **eDiscovery** (`/api/compliance/search|export`) for matters, clients, documents, time, trust entries and conflict checks. Deletes under a legal hold are kept, hidden.

Not yet ported from SynergyLaw: legal research (CourtListener) and its legal knowledge library.

Port 3870, database `law`. Tests run against Postgres when `TEST_DATABASE_URL` is set: `npm test --workspace addons/law`.
